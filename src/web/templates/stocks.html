How Trading Recommendations Are Calculated
Enhanced Multi-Strategy System

Our trading recommendations use a sophisticated approach that generates and tests multiple strategies to find the best option for each stock.

For each stock analysis, we generate 5 different trading strategies with varying risk profiles:

Step 2: Historical Backtesting

Each strategy is tested against {{ historical_lookback_days }} days of real market data from Alpha Vantage:

  • Win Rate Calculation: Percentage of profitable trades
  • Average Return: Mean profit/loss per trade
  • Consistency Score: Standard deviation of returns
  • Risk-Adjusted Return: Sharpe ratio calculation
Step 3: Confidence Scoring

Final confidence combines multiple factors:

Sentiment Analysis (30%)
  • News sentiment score
  • Confidence level
  • News volume
Historical Performance (70%)
  • Strategy win rate
  • Average returns
  • Consistency score
  • Market conditions match
Step 4: Strategy Selection

The system selects the strategy with the highest overall confidence score and provides:

  • Specific strike price recommendations
  • Optimal expiry dates
  • Position sizing suggestions
  • Risk management guidelines
